                A quiet promise

                Your resume is personal.
                Your analysis is temporary.

                • Read once, then deleted. The temporary copy of your file is removed as soon as your report is generated.
                • AI does the reading. To produce the analysis, your resume text is sent to Google's Gemini API — Google's own data policies apply to that processing.
                • No accounts, no history. This app keeps no database of uploads and no record of your reports.
                • Share thoughtfully. Avoid uploading details you wouldn't want processed by a third-party AI service.
                handled with care

                About this tool

                AI Resume Analyzer is an MCA project built with Flask and Google Gemini. It reads PDF, DOCX and TXT resumes, scores them the way an applicant tracking system would, and turns the findings into a practical, human-readable career review — no dashboards full of noise, just the things worth fixing.
